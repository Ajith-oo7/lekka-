LEKKA: THE WHOLE APP EXPLAINED, FROM A TO Z
===========================================

"Lekka" means "account" or "count" in Telugu. The app keeps a clear,
shared account of money lent between people who know each other.

Status of this code (November 2026): Phases 1 to 6 are written. The
database rules are tested. The phone app itself has not yet been compiled
or run on a device, so expect some fixes the first time it is built.

1. WHAT LEKKA IS
----------------
Lekka is a mobile app for Android and iPhone. It replaces the notebook
that a moneylender keeps. Every loan, every payment, every rupee of
interest and every change of the interest rate is written down in a list
called Records. Nothing in Records can be edited or deleted afterwards.

The lender and the borrower can both open the same loan on their own
phones and see the same numbers.

Lekka only keeps records and sends reminders. It never moves money.
People still pay each other in cash or by their usual method, and then
write the payment in Lekka.

Languages: English, Telugu and Hindi. Currency: Indian rupees, shown
with Indian grouping (12,34,567).


2. THE PROBLEM IT SOLVES (USE CASE)
-----------------------------------
In South India many people lend money informally to family, neighbours
and people in their village. This is called vaddi vyaparam. The lender
writes the loans in a notebook. This causes problems:

  - Only the lender has the notebook. The borrower has to trust it.
  - Interest is worked out by hand, so mistakes and arguments happen.
  - If the rate changes, there is no proof of when or by how much.
  - Nobody remembers due dates until it is too late.
  - A lost notebook means lost records.

Lekka gives both sides the same record. Interest is added by the app
from the agreed terms, each amount comes with a short explanation, and
both people are reminded before the due date.


3. WHO USES IT, AND THE THREE WAYS TO USE IT
--------------------------------------------
There are two kinds of people: the lender (gives the money) and the
borrower (takes it). One person can be a lender on some loans and a
borrower on others. The home screen has a switch: "Money Lent" and
"Money Borrowed".

Three ways to use it:

  a) Shared loan. Both people have the app. The lender creates the loan
     and invites the borrower with a code. Both see the same loan. The
     borrower accepts the terms and confirms payments.

  b) Solo lender. The borrower has no phone or no app. The lender adds
     the borrower as a name and tracks the loan alone.

  c) Solo borrower. A person records a loan they took from someone who
     does not use the app, to keep their own account of it.

The lender always controls the interest terms. In a shared loan the
borrower cannot change them, only accept them. Every change is a new
"version" of the terms and is shown in Records.


4. A STORY: BALA LENDS VASU 500 RUPEES
--------------------------------------
Day 1. Bala lends Vasu 500 rupees at 10% interest per month, to be
repaid in 30 days. Bala opens Lekka, taps "New Loan", picks his group
"Family", adds Vasu as the borrower, types 500 and 10, and saves.
Records now shows two lines: "Loan given 500" and "Terms updated:
version 1, 10% per month, simple".

Bala taps "Invite Vasu to this loan" and gets a code. He sends it to
Vasu. Vasu installs Lekka, signs up, opens "Money Borrowed", taps
"Join with code" and types it. Vasu now sees the same loan. A card asks
him to accept the terms. He reads "10% per month, Simple" and taps
"Accept terms". Bala's phone shows "Vasu accepted the terms".

Every night. At 00:30 India time the server adds one day of interest to
every active loan. For this loan that is 1.67 rupees a day. Each line in
Records says how it was worked out: "Simple interest: 10% per month on
500".

Day 10. Vasu pays Bala 100 rupees in cash. Bala taps "Record payment"
and types 100. By then 16.70 of interest has built up. The payment
clears the interest first (16.70) and the rest (83.30) reduces the
loan. From the next day, interest is charged on 416.70, not 500. Vasu
opens Records and taps "Confirm payment". Bala sees "Confirmed by
borrower".

A mistake. Suppose Bala typed 1000 instead of 100. He cannot edit the
line. He presses and holds the wrong payment and chooses "Add
correction". The wrong line stays, with a line through it, and a new
"Correction" line cancels it. Then he records the right amount. Both
people can see exactly what happened.

Day 28. Two days before the due date, Vasu's phone shows "Payment due
in 2 days". On day 30 it shows "Payment due today".

Day 31 onwards. If Vasu has not paid, he gets "Payment overdue" every
day. If Bala set late interest, it is now added each day and the
reminder says how much late interest has been added so far. Bala can
also tap "Send ping" to nudge Vasu, up to 3 times a day.

A new rate. Bala and Vasu agree on 8% from now on. Bala taps "Change
terms". Lekka saves version 2. The days already passed keep 10%; days
from today use 8%. Records shows "Terms updated: version 2".

The end. When Vasu has paid everything, the balance is 0 and the loan
shows "Paid". Bala taps "Close loan". The full history stays visible to
both of them.


5. WHAT THE APP CAN DO (FUNCTIONS)
----------------------------------
Accounts
  - Sign up and sign in with email and password, and your name.
  - Choose English, Telugu or Hindi. Change it any time in Settings.
  - Light mode, dark mode, or the same as the phone.

Loans
  - Groups: put loans into named groups (for example "Family").
  - People: add a borrower (or lender) with a name and optional phone.
  - New loan: amount, interest %, period (day, week, month, year),
    simple or compound interest, late interest %, grace days, start
    date, due date.
  - Loan page: balance, status, terms, and that loan's Records.
  - Record a payment, with date and optional note.
  - Correct a wrong payment (press and hold it).
  - Change terms (creates a new version).
  - Close a loan.

Sharing
  - Invite the borrower with a 10-character code (works once, 7 days).
  - Borrower joins with the code, accepts terms, confirms payments.
  - Both phones update within seconds of a change.

Interest
  - Added automatically every night by the server.
  - Late interest after the due date plus grace days.
  - Every amount has a plain-language note.

Reminders
  - Push notifications: 2 days before, on the due date, every day
    while overdue.
  - Manual ping from the lender (3 a day per loan).
  - A Reminders screen listing everything received.
  - In the receiver's own language.

Overview
  - Home: total to receive or repay, number of active loans, expected
    monthly interest, late interest added, loans sorted by due date.
  - Records: every entry across all loans.
  - Summary: totals lent and borrowed, interest earned, loans by status.

Other
  - Report a problem (Settings), which reaches the app owner.
  - Chits: a placeholder that says "Coming soon".


6. HOW TO USE IT, STEP BY STEP
------------------------------
As a lender
  1. Sign up. Pick your language.
  2. Tap "New Loan" (green button on the home screen).
  3. Choose a group, or make a new one.
  4. Choose the borrower, or "Add a new person".
  5. Type the amount and the interest rate. Pick the period and whether
     interest is simple or compound. Add late interest and grace days
     if you want them (0 means none). Set the start and due dates.
  6. Tap "Save loan".
  7. If the borrower has a phone: open the loan, tap "Invite ... to
     this loan", and send them the code.
  8. When they pay you, open the loan and tap "Record payment".
  9. Made a mistake? Press and hold the payment, then "Add correction".
 10. To change the rate, tap "Change terms".
 11. When it is fully paid, tap "Close loan".

As a borrower who was invited
  1. Sign up. Open "Money Borrowed" on the home screen.
  2. Tap "Join with code" and type the code the lender sent.
  3. Read the terms and tap "Accept terms".
  4. Open the loan any time to see the balance and Records.
  5. When the lender records your payment, tap "Confirm payment".

As a borrower keeping your own account
  1. Open "Money Borrowed", tap "New Loan".
  2. Choose "I took this loan" and add the lender's name.
  3. Fill in the rest as above. Only you can see this loan.

8. THE RULES AND CALCULATIONS (ALGORITHMS)
------------------------------------------
8.1 Money is stored as whole paise
    1 rupee = 100 paise. 500 rupees is stored as 50000. The app never
    uses decimal fractions for money, so there are no rounding
    surprises. Interest rates are stored as "basis points": 10.00% is
    stored as 1000.

8.2 Records are append-only
    A line in Records can never be changed or deleted. The database
    itself refuses. A mistake is fixed by adding a "Correction" line
    that cancels the wrong one. Each line can be corrected only once.

8.3 The balance
    Balance = loan given + interest added + late interest added
              - payments + corrected payments.
    It is always calculated from Records, never typed in.

8.4 Period lengths
    Day = 1 day, week = 7, month = 30, year = 365.
    Daily rate = rate / period length.
    Example: 10% per month = 10 / 30 = 0.3333% per day.

8.5 Simple interest
    Each day: interest = principal still owed x daily rate.
    Example: 500 x 10% / 30 = 1.67 per day. After 30 days: 550.10.

8.6 Compound interest
    Each day: interest = (principal still owed + unpaid interest)
                         x daily rate.
    Unpaid interest earns interest too. The same loan after 30 days:
    552.50.

8.7 Payments: interest first
    A payment first clears unpaid interest. Only what is left reduces
    the principal. (Day 10 in the story: 100 paid, 16.70 to interest,
    83.30 to principal.)

8.8 Late interest
    Starts the day after: due date + grace days. Each day while the
    balance is above zero: late interest = balance x late rate / period
    length. One entry per day.

8.9 Terms versions
    Changing terms never edits the old ones. A new version is saved.
    It applies from the day it is saved (India time). Earlier days keep
    the earlier version.

8.10 The nightly interest job
    At 00:30 India time the server goes through every active loan. For
    each loan it walks day by day from the start date:
      - applies that day's payments and corrections,
      - finds the terms version in force that day,
      - if that day's interest is already in Records, uses it as is,
      - if not, calculates it and adds it.
    This means: running the job twice adds nothing the second time, and
    if the job misses some days it fills them in on its next run.
    Interest for a day is added after that day ends.

8.11 Rounding
    Each day's interest is rounded to the nearest paisa (half rounds
    up). Over a month this can differ from the exact figure by a few
    paise: 550.10 instead of 550.00 in the example.

8.12 Loan status
    Paid: balance is 0. Overdue: past the due date. Due soon: due
    today or within 2 days. Otherwise: On track.

8.13 Reminders
    Once a day at 09:00 India time, for each active loan with a balance:
    2 days before the due date, on the due date, and every day after
    it. The same kind is never sent twice in one day. The reminder goes
    to the borrower. If the borrower has no app, it goes to the lender.

8.14 Pings
    Only the lender of a shared loan can ping, at most 3 times per loan
    per day. The limit is enforced on the server.

8.15 Invite codes
    10 random characters, shown as XXXXX-XXXXX. A code works once and
    expires after 7 days. The lender cannot use their own code.

8.16 Who can see what
    Every table in the database has a rule: you can read a loan only
    if you created it, lent it, or borrowed it. A third person sees
    nothing. Only the lender (or the person keeping a solo record) can
    add payments or change terms. Only the borrower can accept terms
    and confirm payments. Only the server can add interest.


9. HOW IT IS BUILT (THE PARTS)
------------------------------
  The phone app     Flutter (one codebase for Android and iPhone).
                    Folder: lib/

  The backend       Supabase: a Postgres database, sign-in, access
                    rules, and live updates. Folder: supabase/

  Server jobs       Two small programs (Edge Functions) started every
                    day by a timer in the database:
                      accrue-interest   adds interest at 00:30
                      send-reminders    sends reminders at 09:00, and
                                        pings when a lender asks

  Push messages     Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to
                    phones.

How a change travels: the app sends it to the database. The database
checks the rules and saves it. The database then tells the other phone,
which reloads. Interest and reminders start from the server's timer,
not from anyone's phone, so they happen even when nobody opens the app.

The important rules (who may see or change what, how interest is
calculated, the ping limit) live in the database, not in the app. Even
a modified app could not break them.


10. WHAT IS STORED (THE DATA)
-----------------------------
  profiles             your name and language
  contacts             people you added (name, optional phone)
  groups               named groups, and who is a member
  loans                amount, dates, who lent, who borrowed, status
  loan_terms           each version of the interest terms
  ledger_entries       Records: loan given, payment, interest, late
                       interest, terms change, correction
  entry_confirmations  payments the borrower confirmed
  invites              invite codes
  notifications        reminders and pings received
  device_tokens        which phone to send your notifications to
  feedback             problem reports from testers

Passwords are handled by the sign-in service. The app never stores or
sees them in readable form.


11. PRIVACY AND SAFETY
----------------------
  - A person sees only loans they are part of.
  - Records cannot be altered, so neither side can rewrite history.
  - The app holds only a public key. The powerful server key stays on
    the server and is never in the app.
  - Names and amounts are not written to logs.
  - The app makes no claims such as "100% secure" and no claims about
    legal compliance. Legal and licensing questions belong to the app
    owner.
  - An independent security review is planned before any public launch.


12. WHAT LEKKA DOES NOT DO
--------------------------
  - It does not send, receive or hold money.
  - It has no payment gateway.
  - It does not take gold or property as security.
  - It has no website version and no admin panel.
  - Sign-in by phone number (OTP) is not built yet.
  - The Chits feature is not built yet.
  - The due date does not move forward by itself. An overdue loan keeps
    getting late interest and a daily reminder until it is paid or
    closed.

14. WORDS USED IN THIS FILE
---------------------------
  Lender         the person who gives the money
  Borrower       the person who takes the money
  Principal      the amount lent, not counting interest
  Interest       the extra money charged for the loan
  Simple         interest charged only on the principal
  Compound       interest charged on principal plus unpaid interest
  Late interest  extra interest charged after the due date
  Grace days     days allowed after the due date before late interest
  Terms          the agreed rate, period, type, late interest, grace days
  Version        one saved set of terms; a change makes a new version
  Records        the permanent list of everything that happened on a loan
  Correction     an entry that cancels a wrong payment
  Balance        what is still owed right now
  Ping           a manual reminder sent by the lender
  Group          a named folder of loans
  Paise          1/100 of a rupee
  Vaddi vyaparam informal moneylending in South India
